Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 29, 2026

Plasticine Games (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is and is not collected when you use Slime Sliders and other Plasticine Games websites and applications.

By accessing or using our website or games, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Who We Are

Plasticine Games is a small independent game studio owned and operated by Shawn.Genji as a sole proprietorship based in Tennessee, United States.

Information Stored on Your Device

Slime Sliders stores certain information locally within your web browser using your browser’s Local Storage to provide game functionality and preserve your progress.

This information may include:

  • Save data and game progress
  • Achievements
  • Unlocked items, cosmetics, or game content
  • High scores and personal bests
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Music and sound effect volume settings
  • Other gameplay information necessary to provide a seamless experience

This information remains only on your device.

Plasticine Games does not receive, collect, upload, or have access to this information.

If you clear your browser’s site data or Local Storage, your saved progress, unlocked content, and preferences may be permanently erased.

Information We Collect

Plasticine Games does not intentionally collect personal information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Home address
  • Telephone number
  • Payment information
  • Government-issued identification
  • Usernames or passwords

Slime Sliders does not require creating an account.

Analytics and Tracking

Plasticine Games does not currently operate its own analytics platform.

We do not intentionally collect gameplay statistics, crash reports, or usage analytics.

Cookies

Plasticine Games does not intentionally use cookies or similar tracking technologies for our own purposes.

However, third-party services integrated into the website, such as advertising providers, may use cookies or similar technologies in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Advertising

Slime Sliders may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense or other advertising partners.

These third-party advertising services may collect information about your device or browsing activity in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

Plasticine Games does not control how these services collect, process, or use information.

For additional information about Google’s data practices, please review Google’s Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

Our website or games may utilize third-party services, including but not limited to:

  • Google AdSense
  • GitHub
  • Instagram

Each third-party provider maintains its own privacy policy and terms of service.

Plasticine Games is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

Children’s Privacy

Plasticine Games does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

If you believe personal information has been submitted by a child, please contact us so that we can make reasonable efforts to address the situation.

Data Security

Because Slime Sliders stores gameplay information locally on your own device instead of on Plasticine Games servers, much of your gameplay data never leaves your browser.

Although we strive to maintain secure development and hosting practices, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You may:

  • Delete your browser’s Local Storage at any time.
  • Disable cookies through your browser settings.
  • Use browser features that block advertising cookies.
  • Install privacy-focused browser extensions if you prefer.

Please note that deleting Local Storage may permanently erase your saved progress, unlocked content, achievements, preferences, and other game data.

International Visitors

Plasticine Games operates from the United States.

If you access the Services from another country, your use of the Services is governed by applicable United States laws, subject to any mandatory protections provided by your local jurisdiction.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Plasticine Games may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as Slime Sliders and future projects evolve.

Any updates will be reflected by revising the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page.

Your continued use of the Services after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.

Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact Plasticine Games through Instagram: @plasticinegames.
